College Information

ABC College of Engineering was established in 1998 and is affiliated to a State Technical University. The college offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in various engineering disciplines.

Our college ranks 1st in the district for placements. The chemical formula taught in our Chemistry lab, H2O, is a common example of subscript usage.
